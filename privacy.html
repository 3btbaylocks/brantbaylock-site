Privacy

A simple privacy standard for a high-trust advisory site.

The public website is designed to collect only what is needed to understand visitor interest, improve the site, and respond to a requested conversation.

Website analytics

BrantBaylock.com uses Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use the site. These services may measure page views, traffic sources, general device and browser information, clicks, scrolling, and engagement. Microsoft Clarity may also create privacy-masked session recordings and heatmaps. Analytics data is used to improve the website and understand interest in advisory content and resources.

The site does not use analytics to collect names, email addresses, resident information, clinical records, insurance details, Social Security numbers, or other sensitive information.

Contact requests

Contact and introduction forms may ask for basic identifying and contact information so Brant can respond. Do not submit resident names, diagnoses, records, insurance details, Social Security numbers, or clinical information through public website forms.

Third-party services

The site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity and links to Google Forms and LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Questions

For privacy questions about this site, contact Brant@BrantBaylock.com.
