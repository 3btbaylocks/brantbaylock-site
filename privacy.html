Privacy

A simple privacy standard for a high-trust advisory site.

The public website is designed to collect only what is needed to understand visitor interest, improve the site, and respond to a requested conversation.

Website analytics

The site may use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity after those services are activated. Those tools can measure visits, page and section engagement, traffic sources, clicks, and general device/browser information. Analytics should not be used to collect resident clinical information or other sensitive information through public pages.

Contact requests

Contact and introduction forms may ask for basic identifying and contact information so Brant can respond. Do not submit resident names, diagnoses, records, insurance details, Social Security numbers, or clinical information through public website forms.

Third-party links

The site links to Google Forms, Google Drive resources, and LinkedIn. Those services have their own privacy practices.

Questions

For privacy questions about this site, contact Brant@BrantBaylock.com.
