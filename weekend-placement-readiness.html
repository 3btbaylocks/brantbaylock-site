Weekend Placement Readiness

A clearer place to start before the weekend.

When Friday comes, referral professionals should not have to guess which communities may be ready to respond. WPR helps participating communities share current weekend readiness before the calls begin.

Participant Sign InAsk about WPR

For participating communities and verified referral professionals.

The problem

Friday should not start with a phone tree.

A referral may be ready to move, but the first challenge is often simply finding out who can respond, assess, or potentially admit over the weekend.

WPR gives participating communities a consistent way to share that readiness, so verified referral professionals have a better starting point and can go directly to the community for the conversation that matters.

A better starting point

WPR is built to make weekend communication clearer before the calls begin. Communities share current readiness, referral professionals know where to start, and the conversation still happens directly with the community.

How it helps

Less guessing. Better first calls.

WPR does not change who makes decisions. It helps the right people get to the right conversation sooner.

01

Share weekend readiness

Participating communities can let verified professionals know when their team may be prepared to respond, assess, or consider an admission.

02

Know where to begin

Referral professionals can start with current, community-confirmed readiness instead of relying on stale lists or assumptions.

03

Keep the decision with the community

Every assessment, fit decision, availability confirmation, and admission remains directly with the community.

What WPR is not

WPR opens the door to a conversation. It does not make the placement decision.

WPR does not rank, recommend, or endorse communities. It is not a family-placement service. Referral professionals contact communities directly, and communities retain full authority over assessment and admission.
