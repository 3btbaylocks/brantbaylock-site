Weekend Placement Readiness

Current readiness, without another complicated system.

Weekend Placement Readiness is being designed to help independent communities share current, community-approved Friday readiness information with verified referral professionals before the weekend begins.

The problem

Friday can create repeated calls, stale information, and dead ends.

The program is intended to create a more useful starting point before professionals call communities directly. Direct confirmation still decides.

Controlled development

WPR is being built and tested as a controlled pilot. Public onboarding, participant access, protected contacts, and live Friday availability are not open on this website.

For independent communities

One permanent profile. One brief Friday update.

The community stays in control of what it publishes, whom it speaks with, whom it assesses, and every admission decision.

Current information

A useful Friday snapshot

Current bed count, room or resident type, general acuity snapshot, weekend capability, temporary restrictions, monitored contact, and confirmation time.

Community control

No change to admission authority

The community confirms its information, speaks directly with the professional, evaluates the prospective resident, and makes every admission decision.

Privacy

Resident information stays outside WPR

No resident names, diagnoses, records, insurance details, or clinical documents belong in the readiness process.

Discuss community participation
For discharge & placement professionals

A faster starting point. Direct confirmation still decides.

A future Friday readiness report may provide current, community-approved starting information before the professional calls communities directly.

Reduce dead ends

Start with current Friday information.

Know which participating communities recently confirmed readiness before spending time on repeated first calls.

Professional boundary

Not a placement agency.

The program does not approve placement, replace assessment or documentation, determine financial fit, or override admission decisions.

Confirmation

Every listing remains a starting point.

Availability and requirements must be confirmed directly with the community before any placement decision.

Request a listening conversation

Small enough to learn. Structured enough to trust.

The pilot is designed to test whether one weekly readiness process can reduce Friday placement friction without changing admission authority, collecting resident information, or promising referrals.
