About Brant

Senior living community advisory, with brokerage available when the decision requires it.

The work begins before a transaction. The focus is helping privately held senior living owners understand the community, the market, the pressure points, and the options around a major decision.

How I fit

Owner-side perspective before the market takes over.

That can mean an early clarity conversation, a second look at value, a facility-side interpretation of buyer or lender questions, market intelligence, or preparation before a refinance, succession, expansion, or sale.

  • Privately held assisted living and memory care communities
  • Florida's Central-East Corridor
  • Performance, value, risk, readiness, succession, and transition
  • Facility Impact Report and FIRst Step
  • The Corridor market intelligence
  • Weekend Placement Readiness program development

Working principles

Useful before urgent.

The objective is not to manufacture a transaction. It is to improve the owner's understanding before timing, pressure, or another party defines the decision.

Clarity

Understand the real question.

Separate the community issue from the transaction assumption.

Context

Read the community and the market together.

Operating facts mean more when viewed through buyer, lender, operator, and submarket lenses.

Control

Give ownership more options.

Preparation is valuable because it allows owners to choose rather than react.
