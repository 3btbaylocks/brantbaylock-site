Owner Advisory

Understand the community before buyers, lenders, or the market define it.

I help owners of privately held assisted living and memory care communities understand performance, value, risk, and what that position may allow them to do next before a major decision becomes time-sensitive.

Where I fit

The work begins before a transaction.

An owner may be dealing with fatigue, family timing, census pressure, capital needs, succession questions, a quiet offer, or uncertainty around what the community is really worth. The first task is to help the owner understand the issue and the options actually available.

  • Owner fatigue, capacity, or family timing
  • Census, labor, margin, or capital pressure
  • Unsolicited offer or pricing question
  • Refinance, expansion, succession, or sale consideration
  • A community story that needs clarity before the market judges it
What clarity can make possible

Value is useful when it creates options.

A number by itself is not the destination. A clearer view of performance, risk, and value can help ownership see which choices are realistic and which need more work first.

HoldContinue operating with a clearer view of what is working and what must stay durable.
StrengthenReinvest selectively or improve operations where the effort can meaningfully change the picture.
PrepareClarify what needs to be true before a refinance, capital, succession, or future sale conversation with the appropriate professionals.
TransitionPosition the community for a cleaner succession, sale, recapitalization, or other change when ownership is ready.
Advisory tools

Two levels of community clarity.

FIRst Step is the low-friction entry point, using limited owner input to provide useful perspective without a document dump. The Facility Impact Report goes deeper when ownership decides a more complete diagnostic is warranted.
