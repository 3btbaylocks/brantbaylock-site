Owner Advisory

Understand the community before buyers, lenders, or the market define it.

I help owners of privately held assisted living and memory care communities understand performance, value, risks, and readiness so they can decide whether to hold, strengthen, prepare, or transition.

Where I fit

The work begins before a transaction.

An owner may be dealing with fatigue, family timing, census pressure, capital needs, succession questions, a quiet offer, or uncertainty around what the community is really worth. The first task is to help the owner understand the issue and the options actually available.

  • Owner fatigue, capacity, or family timing
  • Census, labor, margin, or capital pressure
  • Unsolicited offer or pricing question
  • Refinance, expansion, succession, or sale consideration
  • A facility story that needs clarity before the market judges it
Decision framework

Hold. Strengthen. Prepare. Transition.

The best path is the one that fits ownership's goals and timing, not the one a transaction process assumes.

HoldProtect the strengths that make continued ownership worthwhile.
StrengthenAddress pressure before it changes value or optionality.
PrepareBuild clarity before a decision becomes time-sensitive.
TransitionPosition the community for a cleaner change when ownership is ready.
