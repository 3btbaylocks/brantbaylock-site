FIRst Step

The first step to community clarity.

A preliminary signal brief designed to help independent senior living owners understand how their community may be read by buyers, lenders, and operators before a listing decision, pricing conversation, or transition commitment.

What it helps clarify

An early read, not a valuation.

  • Early operating and market signals
  • Potential pressure points
  • Buyer, lender, and operator interpretation
  • Whether a deeper community review is warranted
What it is not

FIRst Step is not an appraisal, a full valuation, a BOV, or a listing commitment. Its purpose is to help ownership decide what deserves a closer look before making a larger decision.

Owner inputs

A focused starting set.

Enough information to form a preliminary signal without turning the first conversation into full diligence.

Community

Licensed beds + census

Basic scale, current occupancy, and how much capacity is actually being used.

Operations

Revenue + staffing model

Early context around operating performance, labor pressure, and ownership dependence.

Ownership

Objective + timing

What ownership is trying to understand, what has changed, and whether timing is beginning to matter.
