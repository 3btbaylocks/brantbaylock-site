FIRst Step

The first step to facility clarity.

A preliminary facility signal brief designed to help independent senior living owners understand how their community may be read by buyers, lenders, and operators before a listing decision, pricing conversation, or transition commitment.

What it helps clarify

An early read, not a valuation.

  • Early facility signals
  • Potential pressure points
  • Buyer, lender, and operator interpretation
  • Whether deeper facility review is warranted
What it is not

Not an appraisal. Not a full valuation. Not a BOV. Not a listing commitment. The next step is a conversation, not a commitment.

Owner inputs

A focused starting set.

Enough information to form a preliminary signal without turning the first step into full diligence.

Facility

Licensed beds + census

Basic scale and current occupancy.

Operations

Revenue + staffing model

Early operating context and pressure.

Ownership

Objective + timing

What the owner is actually trying to decide and any known concerns.
