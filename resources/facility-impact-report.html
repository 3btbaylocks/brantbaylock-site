Facility Impact Report · FIR

A deeper diagnostic for community performance, value pressure, and transition planning.

The Facility Impact Report, or FIR, helps ownership understand how community performance, market position, buyer fit, lender confidence, and transition readiness may affect value and decision-making before a sale process, pricing conversation, refinance, or major strategic move.

Core review areas

What supports value. What creates pressure.

The FIR looks beyond a headline price and organizes the community story through the lenses buyers, lenders, and replacement operators may use.

Performance

Census + revenue efficiency

Where performance sits against stabilization and how revenue traction reads relative to available capacity.

Durability

Labor + operational stability

Whether staffing, leadership depth, and ownership dependence create confidence or transfer risk.

Marketability

Buyer fit + financeability

Which buyer pool is realistic, how lenders may read the story, and where underwriting may apply pressure.

Owner receives

Decision support, not just a number.

  • Executive summary and community snapshot
  • Performance gap review
  • Revenue and labor efficiency read
  • Buyer profile alignment
  • Value support vs. pressure analysis
  • Decision support and next-step priorities
Boundary

The FIR is not an appraisal, a guaranteed value conclusion, a listing requirement, or legal, tax, financial, or clinical advice. It is an owner-side framework for understanding the story before the market defines it.
