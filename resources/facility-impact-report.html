Facility Impact Report · FIR

A private diagnostic for owners facing a consequential decision.

FIR is designed to help ownership understand whether the community story supports confidence, creates pressure, or needs work before a sale, refinance, succession decision, reinvestment, or other major transition.

What FIR helps answer

What is helping the story, and what could weaken it?

The value is not a public checklist or a formula. It is the owner-side interpretation of how the community may be read by the market and what that means for timing, leverage, and available options.

Position

Does the community support the story ownership wants to tell?

FIR helps separate what is genuinely supporting value from what may only look strong from inside the operation.

Perception

Where could a buyer or lender become cautious?

Understanding likely pressure points early gives ownership more time to decide whether to address them, explain them, or price around them.

Options

What deserves attention before a major move?

The goal is better decision timing and better preparation, not forcing a sale or producing a headline number.

Private work product

The methodology stays inside the advisory relationship.

The detailed review structure, owner inputs, analysis framework, and report format are not published on the website. FIR is prepared in context for the individual community and ownership objective.

Boundary

FIR is not an appraisal, a guaranteed value conclusion, a listing requirement, or legal, tax, financial, or clinical advice. It is private decision support for ownership.
