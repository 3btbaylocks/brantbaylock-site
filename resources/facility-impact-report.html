Facility Impact Report

A deeper diagnostic for facility performance, value pressure, and transition planning.

A deeper advisory report designed to help ownership understand how facility performance, market position, buyer fit, lender confidence, and transition readiness may affect value and decision-making before a sales process, pricing conversation, or major strategic move.

Core review areas

What supports value. What creates pressure.

The FIR looks beyond a headline price and organizes the facility story through the lenses buyers, lenders, and replacement operators may use.

Performance

Census + revenue efficiency

Where performance sits against stabilization and how revenue traction reads.

Durability

Labor + operational stability

Whether staffing and ownership dependence create confidence or risk.

Marketability

Buyer fit + financeability

Which buyer pool is realistic and where underwriting may apply pressure.

Owner receives

Decision support, not just a number.

  • Executive summary and facility snapshot
  • Performance gap review
  • Revenue and labor efficiency read
  • Buyer profile alignment
  • Value support vs. pressure analysis
  • Decision support and next-step priorities
Boundary

The FIR is not an appraisal, not a guaranteed value conclusion, not a listing requirement, and not legal, tax, or financial advice.
